# Support

Questions, bugs or feature ideas: email
[lk.khatri558@gmail.com](mailto:lk.khatri558@gmail.com), or use **Send
feedback** in the app's ••• menu, which fills in your app and iOS version.

**Subscriptions.** Manage or cancel CaptionsAI Pro in Settings › Apple ID ›
Subscriptions. To get Pro back on a new phone, open the paywall and tap
**Restore Purchases**.

**Languages other than English** use a one-time speech-model download
(466 MB, or 142 MB on older iPhones). Wi-Fi is recommended.

See also the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
